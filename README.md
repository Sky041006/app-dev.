# My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

**Genre:** Animation, Action, Adventure  
**Release Year:** 2018  
**Main Character:** Miles Morales

### About the Movie

*Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* follows Miles Morales, a teenager
who gains spider powers and meets other Spider-People from different
universes. Together, they try to stop a threat that could destroy their worlds.

### Why I Like It

- The animation looks like a comic book in motion.
- Miles feels relatable because he doubts himself while learning to be a hero.
- The soundtrack makes the action scenes more exciting.

### My Top 3 Moments

1. Miles meets Spider-People from other universes.
2. Miles takes his leap of faith.
3. The Spider-People work together in the final battle.

> My takeaway: Sometimes you have to take the first step before you feel ready.
